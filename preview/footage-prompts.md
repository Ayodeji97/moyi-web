# Mọyì website footage: prompts and shot list

The mood is evening and warm lamplight, moving from day into night as the reader scrolls. Every clip:

- has no logos, no text on screen, no visible brand on the phone, and no faces looking at the camera;
- is shot or graded warm, with soft contrast and gentle film grain;
- loops without a visible cut where it is marked "loop".

The app's own screens are **never generated**. Wherever a phone screen shows, it is blank (dark) in the footage, and the real app recording is laid over it in code. That keeps the app honest, and lets us update the screens without re-shooting.

These prompts work in Higgsfield (Kling 3.0, Seedance), Google Veo, OpenAI Sora or Runway. If you film them yourself instead, each prompt doubles as a shot description.

---

## 1. Hero loop, desktop (16:9, 8–10 s, loop)
> Close-up of a hand holding a modern smartphone upright, screen facing the camera and fully dark, in a warm living room at night. A single table lamp and soft string lights glow out of focus behind it, as large round amber bokeh. Shallow depth of field, the phone's edges in sharp focus. Very slow, subtle handheld drift and a soft breathing motion. Warm amber and deep brown colour grade, natural skin tone, gentle film grain. Calm, intimate, quiet. No text, no logos, no face visible. Seamless loop.

## 2. Hero loop, phone (9:16, 8–10 s, loop)
Same as shot 1, framed vertically, with the phone filling the middle third of the frame and room for a headline above it.

## 3. Day into night sky (16:9, 12–15 s, scrubbed by scroll)
> Static wide shot of a city skyline from a rooftop, time-lapse from late golden afternoon to blue hour to night. Warm windows switch on one by one. Soft clouds drift. No people, no text, no aircraft. Smooth exposure transition, warm grade.

The page ties this clip to the scroll position, so the sky darkens as the reader moves down the story of one day.

## 4. Lagos, evening (16:9, 6–8 s, loop)
> A woman in her late twenties sits on a balcony in Lagos at dusk, holding her phone and reading. A small, private smile. Warm tropical evening light, distant city lights, a plant on the balcony rail. Shot from the side, face partly turned away, shallow depth of field. Natural, unposed. No text, no logos.

## 5. London, evening (16:9, 6–8 s, loop)
> A man in his early thirties sits by a rain-streaked window on a London bus at night, looking down at his phone with a quiet smile. City lights blur outside. Cool blue streetlight mixed with warm interior light. Side angle, face partly hidden, shallow depth of field. Natural, unposed. No text, no logos.

## 6. Bedside, the reveal (16:9, 6 s, loop)
> Top-down view of a wooden bedside table at night: a phone lying face up with a dark screen, a cup of tea steaming gently and a small warm lamp. The phone screen softly lights up once. Warm amber light, slow motion, film grain. No text, no logos.

Leave the screen blank in this shot; the real reveal screen is laid over it in code.

## If you film them yourself
- Use any recent iPhone or Android, with **Cinematic mode or Portrait video** for the soft background.
- Shoot in **4K at 24 or 25 fps**. Keep the phone being filmed on a **plain dark screen** (a black image in full screen works).
- Hold each shot for at least 15 seconds so a clean loop can be cut.
- Use one warm lamp as the only light source.
- Send the raw files and I will cut the loops, grade them and compress them for the web (under about 3 MB per clip).
