This page summarises the [Privacy Policy](/privacy). The policy is what you agree to. This page is not a contract.

## In one minute

- **What:** your account, your bond, what you write, your devices' push tokens, and your reports and support messages.
- **Why:** to run Mọyì for you, and to keep it safe.
- **Who sees your words:** the other member of your bond. We look at content only when it has been reported, or when the law requires it. Entries are not end to end encrypted.
- **Never:** ads, tracking, cookies, or selling your data. There are no analytics today.

## Companies that process your data

| Company | Purpose | Data | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hetzner Online | Server and database | Everything stored by Mọyì | Nuremberg, Germany |
| Cloudflare | Media and backup storage; website hosting; DNS | Photos, voice notes, exports, encrypted backups | EU |
| Resend | Account emails | Email address, your name, email content | United States |
| Google Firebase Cloud Messaging | Notifications | Push token, notification type and id | United States |
| Apple Push Notification service | Notifications on iPhone | Push token, notification type and id | United States |
| Amazon Web Services | Automatic photo checks | A smaller copy of each photo | Frankfurt, Germany |

We keep this list current. When a company is added, it appears here first.

## How long

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Account and entries | Until you delete them or your account |
| Full size photos | 90 days, then a smaller copy |
| Archive exports | 24 hours |
| Support messages | Until your request is closed |
| Backups | Up to 35 days |

## Your choices

- **Export:** Bond settings, then Account, then Export your archive.
- **Delete:** Bond settings, then Account, then Delete your account. See [Delete your account](/delete-account).
- **Ask:** [hello@getmoyi.app](mailto:hello@getmoyi.app).
