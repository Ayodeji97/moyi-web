---
title: Delete your Mọyì account
lede: How to delete your account and data, what goes, and what stays.
---

## In the app

1. Open Bond settings, then Account.
2. Tap Delete your account.
3. Type your password and tap Delete my account.

Nothing is deleted that day. Your account is deleted 14 days later, and until then you can still sign in and tap Keep my account to call it off. Want a copy first? Tap Export your archive first.

## Without the app

Email [hello@getmoyi.app](mailto:hello@getmoyi.app) from the address on your account, with the subject **Delete my account**. We reply to that address to confirm it is you, then delete the account within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your email address, name, password, sessions, devices and push tokens.
- Everything you wrote, and your photos and voice notes. Days you shared keep a mark that something was there, so the other person's streak survives.
- Your favourites, reactions, exports and open support messages.
- Your bonds end. The other person sees "Former member" and keeps what they wrote.

## What is kept

- Records without content, such as that a report or block happened, kept by account number to keep Mọyì safe.
- Backups, which expire within 35 days.
- Anything under an open safety report, until a person has reviewed it.

To delete only some of your data, delete an entry from its card in the app, or write to us.
